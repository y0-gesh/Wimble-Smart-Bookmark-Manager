Wimble - Smart Bookmark Manager

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Wimble is a Chrome extension designed to help users organize, categorize, search, and manage their browser bookmarks.

We respect user privacy and aim to minimize data collection. Wimble is designed to process and store bookmark information locally in the user's browser.

Local-Only Guarantee: Wimble does not send your bookmarks, scanned website data, or browsing activity to any external server or third party. Everything is processed directly on your computer.

2. Information We Access

Wimble accesses the following information through Chrome's Bookmarks API:

  • Bookmark titles
  • Bookmark URLs
  • Bookmark folder structure
  • Bookmark identifiers required for bookmark management

This information is used exclusively to provide bookmark organization, categorization, search, backup, restore, and import/export functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Bookmark information is used to:

  • Display and organize saved bookmarks.
  • Automatically categorize bookmarks.
  • Search and retrieve saved bookmarks.
  • Manage bookmark folders and entries.
  • Support backup, restoration, and import/export functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

Wimble stores its extension data locally using Chrome's storage facilities (chrome.storage.local).

Bookmark organization information and extension preferences remain in the user's browser.

Wimble does not require users to create an account or provide personal identification information.

5. Data Sharing

Wimble does not sell, rent, or share users' bookmark information with third parties.

Bookmark information is not transmitted to external servers by Wimble under its local-only operating model.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Wimble's locally stored information remains in the browser until it is deleted by the user or removed through browser or extension data management.

Users can remove the extension at any time to stop using Wimble and clear its associated extension data through Chrome's extension data management.

7. Third-Party Services

Wimble does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services under its local-only operating model.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Wimble's functionality or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.
